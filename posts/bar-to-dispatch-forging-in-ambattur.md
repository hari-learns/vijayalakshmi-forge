Every forged part we ship takes the same route, under one management. Here is what happens at each step, and the equipment behind it.

## Raw material

We forge carbon, alloy and stainless steel. The steel is selected for the job the part has to do, because a forging can only be as good as the bar it starts from.

## Cutting

Bar and billet are cut to forging weight on a bandsaw that takes sections up to 350 mm, or sheared, for smaller sections up to 125 mm. An accurate weight means less waste and a consistent part.

## Heating and hot forging

The cut piece is heated and forged under a hammer of up to 3.5 ton. Controlled forging gives the part its shape and its strength. Our bogie and bell furnace handles heating and thermal processing of forgings, and shot blasting cleans the scale off afterwards.

## Machining

Faces, bores and threads that need precision are turned and milled on CNC and VMC machines. Much of this is done at our sister concern, K.K. Industries, nearby in Ambattur Industrial Estate, so parts do not travel far between steps.

## Inspection

Parts are checked at every critical stage. Magnetic particle inspection finds surface defects that the eye cannot see, and our lab has a hardness tester and a metallurgical microscope.

## Dispatch

Finished parts are packed and sent to the customer's specification. That route has earned supplier awards from Sundram Fasteners (2007), Madras Engineering Industries (2015 and 2021-22) and TIDC India (2020-21).

## Two plants and a machine shop

Plant I is at TASS Industrial Estate, Ambattur. Plant II is at SIDCO Industrial Estate, Kakkalur, Thiruvallur. K.K. Industries is in Ambattur Industrial Estate. All three are certified to ISO 9001:2015.
