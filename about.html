About

About us

Vijayalakshmi Forge and Stamping is an ISO 9001:2015 certified Chennai company that forges carbon, alloy and stainless steel.

It was established by Mr. A. Kumar in 1988 and has grown into a well known name for precision, perfection and performance.

Today the company is led by Mr. A. Kumar, Managing Director, with the support of experienced operational leadership, including Mr. R. Ganesh, GM, Operations.

Alongside the two forging plants, our sister concern K.K. Industries machines components in Ambattur, so forged parts can be finished and inspected close by.
