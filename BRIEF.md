# Brief: Vijayalakshmi Forge & Stamping (upgradeweb, Nov 2026)

1. **Visitor:** a buyer, maintenance or sourcing engineer at a chain, mixer or
   earth-moving equipment maker, in India or abroad. Laptop at work, then a
   phone for WhatsApp. Arrives from a search like "forging company Chennai",
   "conveyor chain link manufacturer" or "excavator sprocket forging".
2. **Ten-second job:** find out whether VLF can make *this part*, and send the
   drawing for a quote.
3. **Decision type:** specify and quote.
4. **Proof that settles doubt:** three ISO 9001:2015 certificates (numbers on
   the page), forging since 1988, a 3.5 ton hammer, CNC/VMC, MPI and a lab,
   named customers (TI India, Wheels India, MEI, Schwing Stetter, Flowserve),
   supplier awards 2007 to 2022. No public reviews were found; there is no
   reviews section.
5. **Signature object and verb:** the forging hammer *strikes*; the logo is a
   ram and anvil over the letters VLF. That drives the loader: three blows
   stamp the letters out of a hot billet.
6. **Tool that replaces the first phone call:** the finder row under the hero.
   Describe the part in your words · what it goes into · material · what you
   can send. It names the families that make it, says why, and writes the
   quote message (WhatsApp or the quote form, prefilled).
7. **Evidence inventory:** 30 refurbished product photos on white, 3
   certificates, 6 award photos, 8 customer logos, poster copy. No photos of
   the plant yet (gallery stays a placeholder).
8. **Logo anatomy:** fluted ram and head (symbol), a wide anvil line, the
   letters V L F. Redrawn as vectors for the loader (`mark.py`); the header
   keeps the original raster.
9. **Brand colour:** navy #1b1a6e and royal blue from the K.K. and VLF marks;
   one warm ember orange, used only for hot metal (loader, the hot-forging step).
10. **Market:** Chennai, English first, WhatsApp first. Export enquiries are the
    growth goal.
11. **Left out:** reviews (none public), invented capacities or prices, plant
    photos (not taken yet), section labels and numbered steps.
12. **Risks:** the finder's keyword lists are *written for the pitch* from the
    poster part names; the client should confirm families and materials.
    Blog posts are drafts for the client to approve. "Best in Ambattur" is a
    claim the client must be happy to make.

## Decisions

- **Loader:** forging story. A hot billet slides onto the anvil; the hammer
  (their ram) strikes three times, lower and quicker each time, with sparks
  and a shudder; the second blow stamps V L F out of the billet; the letters
  cool from orange through cherry red to white; the name is stamped letter by
  letter; the screen opens as a six-band press shutter. About 3s, once per
  session, skipped for reduced motion, pure CSS so it cannot hang.
- **Page change:** the same shutter closes and opens, with the white mark.
- **Fonts:** kept Plus Jakarta Sans and DM Sans (the user chose them). Tracking
  set to 0; JetBrains Mono dropped (figures use tabular numerals).
- **Sky:** soft drifting clouds behind the hero (sky on sky) and the closing
  band (blue on navy).
- **Cards:** no borders, solid fills (sky on white, white on sky).
- **SEO:** canonical, Open Graph, JSON-LD (Organization with certificates,
  awards and an offer catalogue, LocalBusiness for each works, breadcrumbs,
  BlogPosting), sitemap, robots that welcome search and AI crawlers, llms.txt,
  IndexNow key and `indexnow.py`, a Markdown blog in `posts/`.
